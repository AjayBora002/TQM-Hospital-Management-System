## Objective

The objective is to develop a practical Hospital Management System and integrate Total Quality Management into its development, operation and improvement.

The baseline system concentrates on reducing patient wait times and preventing record duplication. The assigned quality goal, Q07 — Improve Data Accuracy, will be implemented through input masking, dropdown lists, auto-complete, confirmation modals and audit logs.

## Customer

The proposed primary operational customer is the **Hospital Administration / Front Desk Staff**, because hospital administration is responsible for patient data entry and record management.

### Users

- Hospital Administrator
- Front Desk Receptionist
- Attending Doctor
- Pharmacist
- Billing Staff
- System Administrator

## Project Quality Direction

The software will prevent data entry errors at the source using Poka-Yoke (mistake-proofing) controls, validate all fields before database insertion, confirm destructive operations, and log every change for traceability.
