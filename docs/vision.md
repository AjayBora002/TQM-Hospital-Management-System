# Project Vision: Hospital Management System (HMS)

## Vision Statement
To establish an error-free, trustworthy, and technologically resilient digital healthcare management ecosystem where medical, clinical, and administrative data maintains 100% integrity, zero redundancy, and full auditability across all patient touchpoints.

## Core Pillars
1. **Total Quality Management (TQM) by Design**:
   System processes are architected around prevention rather than post-incident remediation. Data entry workflows include proactive Poka-Yoke safeguards.

2. **Absolute Clinical & Financial Accuracy**:
   Zero tolerance for patient misidentification, invalid medical records, conflicting doctor appointments, or erroneous billing computations.

3. **Deming's Continuous Improvement Cycle (PDCA)**:
   Constant monitoring of data entry error rates, system exception counts, and audit logs to iteratively eliminate process defects.

4. **Transparent Governance and Accountability**:
   Complete traceability of every transaction, record edit, and administrative decision via dual-tier audit logging (relational DB + immutable compliance CSV exports).
